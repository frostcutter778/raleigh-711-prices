When a price changes

Prices are the posted pump board from 7-Eleven’s store locator, before any 7REWARDS discount. Speedway stations are included because 7-Eleven owns them and lists them in the same locator. History is saved on the data branch when a posted price changes, and at least once an hour. GitHub’s free scheduler cannot check more often than every 5 minutes, and a run can start late.
